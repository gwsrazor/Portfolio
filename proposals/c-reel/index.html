UI/UX Design · App

Honouring tradition through modern craft in digital.

The Open carries over 150 years of history. The R&A wanted an app as premium as the event itself, for fans on the links and at home: real-time leaderboards, hole-by-hole scoring and live video, with the editorial depth its heritage demands.

I designed the end-to-end experience, from the tournament hub and course maps to player profiles and notification flows.
