01 — Brief

Honouring tradition through modern craft in digital.

The Open Championship carries over 150 years of history: it's golf's original major, and its digital experience needed to reflect that legacy while serving a mobile-first audience that follows every shot on their phone.

The R&A wanted an app that felt as premium as the event itself, serving fans both on the links and watching from home.

02 — Challenge

Live data, editorial soul.

The objective: design a championship app that combined real-time leaderboard data, hole-by-hole scoring and live video.

All of it with the editorial depth and visual richness that The Open's heritage demands. Every detail, from typography to transitions, had to be deliberate.

“As premium as the event itself, on the links and from the sofa.”

03 — Approach

End-to-end, shot by shot.

I designed the end-to-end experience across the app. The visual language drew from The Open's brand identity while carving out a cleaner digital language faithful to the championship's character.

The scope covered every moment of a fan's tournament day:

  1. Tournament hub
  2. Live leaderboard & scorecards
  3. Interactive course maps
  4. Player profiles
  5. Tickets & onboarding
  6. Push notification flows
