01Brief

Building an OTT platform from the ground up.

When Altice Media acquired premium sports broadcasting rights in France, they needed an entirely new streaming platform. Fast. RMC Sport had to launch as a credible challenger to established players, delivering live channels, on-demand replays, highlights, and documentaries across every screen: mobile, tablet, web, and connected TV.

02Challenge

The brief was ambitious: design a complete OTT ecosystem from scratch, with a visual identity bold enough to hold its own against established streaming incumbents, flexible enough to scale across platforms with fundamentally different interaction paradigms: touch, cursor, and remote control.

03Approach

I directed the creative vision across all platforms, establishing a shared design language that adapted to each context. The TV experience prioritised lean-back browsing and full-screen immersion, while mobile focused on quick-access live scores and one-tap viewing.
