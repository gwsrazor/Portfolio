01Brief

Designing the hospitality companion for the biggest show on earth.

The first FIFA World Cup held in the Middle East demanded a different kind of digital companion. With eight stadiums, millions of international visitors, and a compact host country, fans needed a single app that could handle everything from live match data to stadium wayfinding, transportation, and hospitality services.

02Challenge

The challenge: create a second-screen experience that served both the die-hard football fan and the casual visitor navigating a foreign country during a once-in-a-lifetime event. The app had to work under extreme load, across dozens of languages.

03Approach

I led the creative direction for the entire product, from initial concept through to launch-day polish. The design balanced dense, real-time match information with practical hospitality features like venue maps, food ordering, and transport schedules.
